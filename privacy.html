Peak Architects is committed to safeguarding the privacy of our website visitors and service users. This policy applies where we act as a data controller with respect to your personal data — in other words, where we determine the purposes and means of processing it.

1. What we collect

Usage data about how you use the website; enquiry data you send us through our contact form or by email; correspondence data contained in messages you send us; and notification data if you subscribe to updates.

2. How we use it

To respond to your enquiry, to provide and administer our services, to maintain the security of our website, and to keep proper business records. We rely on your consent, the performance of a contract, or our legitimate interests as the lawful basis, depending on the purpose.

3. Sharing your data

We may disclose personal data to our insurers or professional advisers, and where required by law or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Please do not supply another person's personal data to us unless we prompt you to do so.

4. Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as is necessary for the purpose it was collected, and in line with our legal and professional record-keeping obligations.

5. Your rights

You have the right to access your personal data, to rectification, to erasure, to restrict or object to processing, and to data portability. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

6. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to ensure you get the best experience when browsing. By continuing to use our website you agree to the use of cookies in accordance with this policy.

7. Contact

To exercise any of your rights, or for any question about this policy, email info@peakarchitects.co.uk or write to the Sheffield studio at Broom Hall, 8–10 Broomhall Road, Sheffield S10 2DR.
